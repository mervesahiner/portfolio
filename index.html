About

A Design Practice
Built on Craft

I’m Merve Sahiner, an interior architect and designer creating calm, warm, quietly confident spaces for contemporary life.

With 3+ years of international experience across the United States, France, Cyprus, and Turkey, I have worked on high-end residences, villa developments, multi-unit housing, hospitality concepts, and commercial interiors. My aesthetic sits at the intersection of wabi-sabi warmth, Scandinavian clarity, and Italian modern precision.

I work from concept through coordination: shaping moodboards and spatial layouts, selecting FF&E, materials, and finishes, developing visualizations, and translating design intent into clear, buildable documentation. I care deeply about proportion, junctions, lighting temperature, and the way surfaces meet — the details that make a space feel effortless rather than decorated.

I hold a Bachelor of Interior Architecture from Eastern Mediterranean University and am pursuing an M.Sc. in Architecture at KTO Karatay University. My current research explores sustainable façade materials and bio-composites.

Values

Designing with
Responsibility

I believe good design should not come at the environment's expense. My thesis research at KTO Karatay University focuses on sustainable facade materials and bio-composites — exploring how locally sourced, low-impact materials can meet high-performance architectural standards. This research informs my design practice: I prioritize lifecycle thinking, responsible material sourcing, and finishes that minimize environmental harm without compromising beauty. I am actively pursuing education in LEED and sustainable building certification to bring this commitment into every project I touch.
